# Elite Contractor AI

## Capstone Project Overview

Elite Contractor AI is a contractor management application designed for small contractors and service businesses. The purpose of the app is to help users manage customers, leads, estimates, job tracking, scheduling, expenses, materials, and project information from a single platform.

By combining practical construction experience with modern software development and artificial intelligence, the application aims to improve organization, productivity, and communication for contractors in the field.

## Phase 1 - Core Features

- Customer and lead management
- Job and project tracking
- Estimate and quote generation
- Scheduling and reminders
- Expense and material tracking
- Dashboard overview

## Phase 2 - AI Features

- AI generated estimates and quotes
- AI generated customer emails and text messages
- Business recommendations
- Marketing and flyer generation
- Project planning assistance

## Project Goal

The long-term goal is to create a professional all-in-one business management platform for contractors that combines real construction experience with modern software development and artificial intelligence.

I have over 20 years of experience in construction and contracting. Throughout my career I have seen how difficult it can be for small contractors to manage customers, jobs, estimates, scheduling, expenses, and communication while still performing the work itself.

This project allows me to combine my industry experience with the software development skills I am learning at triOS College.
